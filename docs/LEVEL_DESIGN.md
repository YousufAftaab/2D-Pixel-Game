# Level Design & Progression: Starbound: The Lost Realms

## 1. Design Philosophy

Every stage follows the five-beat platformer pacing curve:
1. **Introduction**: Introduce a mechanic in a safe, controlled environment.
2. **Development**: Combine the mechanic with previously learned skills.
3. **Challenge**: Add pressure, hazards, or moving obstacles.
4. **Mastery**: Require precise execution or clever use of power-ups to find secret Astral Crystals.
5. **Finale**: Deliver a climatic exit sequence or boss confrontation.

---

## 2. World Breakdown & Mechanics

### World 1: Verdant Valley
- **Theme**: Lush alpine hills, emerald moss, ancient stone ruins.
- **Palette**: Forest green, rich earth brown, twilight blue.
- **Levels**:
  - `1-1: Verdant Beginnings`: Safe introduction to running, variable jumping, Star Shards, stomping Walkers, and checkpoints.
  - `1-2: Ruins of Aethel`: Introduces Breakable Blocks, Prism Blocks containing Ember Core, and Jumper enemies.
  - `1-3: Overgrown Canopy`: Introduces moving platforms and aerial Flyer enemies.
  - `1-4: Gateway of the Sentinel (Boss)`: Boss battle with Ancient Sentinel; requires baiting charges into walls and stomping its head during stunned recovery.

### World 2: Sunscar Desert
- **Theme**: Arid desert, sandstone temples, buried machinery.
- **Palette**: Amber, terracotta, pale gold.
- **Levels**:
  - `2-1: Shifting Sands`: Introduces heavy Chargers and stationary Turret Sentries.
  - `2-2: Tomb of the Sun King`: Introduces Shielded enemies (Vanguard) requiring flanking or stomping.
  - `2-3: Dune Citadel`: Vertical platforming over spike hazards with timed projectile crossfire.
  - `2-4: Sunscar Vault (Boss)`: Advanced Sentinel arena with narrower safe platforms.

### World 3: Frostfall
- **Theme**: Glacial caverns, frozen waterfalls, aurora skies.
- **Palette**: Cyan, deep navy, crystalline white.
- **Levels**:
  - `3-1: Glacial Caverns`: Introduction to Sky Core double-jumping across wide icy chasms.
  - `3-2: Frozen Heights`: High-altitude floating platforms and erratic Flyer patterns.
  - `3-3: Crystal Abyss`: Shielded Vanguards and Turrets guarding hidden Astral Crystals.
  - `3-4: Frost Titan's Lair (Boss)`: Boss battle on slippery icy footing.

### World 4: Sky Ruins
- **Theme**: Floating archipelagos, ancient marble bridges, sky currents.
- **Palette**: Azure, cloud white, gold accents.
- **Levels**:
  - `4-1: Floating Archipelago`: Moving platforms and vertical canopy navigation.
  - `4-2: Temple of Winds`: Precision double-jump timing across crumbling bridges.
  - `4-3: Celestial Bridges`: Multi-tiered route choices rewarding skilled platformers.
  - `4-4: Sky Sanctum (Boss)`: Elevated boss platform over open sky void.

### World 5: Ember Depths
- **Theme**: Volcanic chasms, magma falls, obsidian machinery.
- **Palette**: Crimson, magma orange, basalt charcoal.
- **Levels**:
  - `5-1: Magma River`: Lethal lava floor hazards; introduction of Shield Core protection.
  - `5-2: Basalt Foundry`: Timed hazards, tight leaps between lava pools.
  - `5-3: Core Chamber`: Combination of Chargers, Turrets, and narrow stepping stones.
  - `5-4: Lava Titan's Forge (Boss)`: Arena with lava edges and shockwave projectiles.

### World 6: The Lost Realm
- **Theme**: Shattered cosmic void, fractured celestial architecture.
- **Palette**: Cosmic violet, astral gold, starlight cyan.
- **Levels**:
  - `6-1: Fractured Domain`: Gauntlet combining all enemy archetypes and moving platforms.
  - `6-2: Astral Labyrinth`: Secret routing requiring Ember Core and Sky Core mastery.
  - `6-3: Threshold of Eternity`: Final challenge stage testing timing, speed, and precision.
  - `6-4: The Astral Core — The Warden (Final Boss)`:
    - **Phase 1**: Cosmic teleportation and targeted triple astral bolts.
    - **Phase 2**: Circular ring nova attacks and increased speed.
    - **Phase 3**: Enraged spiral bullet curtains and arena screen shake.

## 3. Collectibles & Secrets Distribution

- **Star Shards**: Placed along optimal jump arcs to guide player trajectories and teach jump timings.
- **Astral Crystals**: Exactly 3 secret crystals hidden per stage (72 total in campaign).
  - Crystal 1: Hidden behind breakable blocks.
  - Crystal 2: Positioned on a high, risky optional platform route.
  - Crystal 3: Hidden near the level exit requiring a leap of faith or double jump.
- **Prism Blocks**: Placed at strategic intervals to offer healing or situational power-up upgrades.
